%\textbf{Static shapes.}
Deep learning optimizations have been widely studied. At the operator level, vendor libraries and template-based systems~\cite{nvidia24cublas,chetlur2014cudnn,nvidia22cutlass,tillet2019triton,cheng2025pipethreader} provide highly optimized kernels for common tensor operators. At the graph level, deep learning frameworks~\cite{ansel2024pytorch,niu2021dnnfusion,shi2023welder,zheng2023chimera,wu2025mirage}
%PyTorch~\cite{ansel2024pytorch}, TASO~\cite{jia2019taso}, Rammer~\cite{ma2020rammer}, DNNFusion~\cite{niu2021dnnfusion}, Welder~\cite{shi2023welder}, Chimera~\cite{zheng2023chimera}, Souffle~\cite{xia2024souffle}, and Mirage~\cite{wu2025mirage} 
exploit graph rewriting, operator fusion and global analysis. 
Tensor compilers and auto-schedulers~\cite{chen2018tvm,chen2018autotvm,zheng2020ansor,ragan2013halide}
%, including TVM~\cite{chen2018tvm}, AutoTVM~\cite{chen2018autotvm}, Ansor~\cite{zheng2020ansor}, and Halide~\cite{ragan2013halide}, 
automate schedule generation through loop transformations, cost models, and heuristic search. 
%%At the graph or end-to-end level, PyTorch~\cite{ansel2024pytorch}, TASO~\cite{jia2019taso}, Rammer~\cite{ma2020rammer}, DNNFusion~\cite{niu2021dnnfusion}, Welder~\cite{shi2023welder}, Chimera~\cite{zheng2023chimera}, Souffle~\cite{xia2024souffle}, and Mirage~\cite{wu2025mirage} exploit graph rewriting, operator fusion, global analysis, or multi-level superoptimization.

These approaches typically assume fixed tensor shapes, static fusion rules, or shape-specific tuning results, making them inefficient when runtime shapes change frequently.

%\textbf{Dynamic shapes.}
Recent work has explored dynamic-shape workloads from both operator and compiler perspectives. From the operator perspective, DietCode~\cite{zheng2022dietcode}, MikPoly~\cite{yu2024mikpoly}, FTuner~\cite{mu2024ftuner}, and Helix~\cite{zhou2025helix} reduce dynamic-shape tuning overhead through shape-generic search spaces, runtime micro-kernel composition, uKernel-based tuning, or sample-free compilation. From the compiler perspective, Nimble~\cite{shen2021nimble}, Relax~\cite{lai2025relax}, and CoRA~\cite{fegade2022cora} improve dynamic-shape execution through dispatching, symbolic shape representation, cross-level abstraction, or padding reduction. In contrast, MAGSAT adapts both fusion decisions and kernel configurations to dynamic tensor programs through its holistic design.
