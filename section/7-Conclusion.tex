\section{Conclusion}%0.25 pages
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents MAGSAT, a unified framework for optimizing dynamic tensor programs 
through memory-access-driven operator fusion and shape-adaptive configuration selection. 
%Instead of relying on static operator categories or exhaustive per-shape profiling, 
%MAGSAT analyzes data-dependence and memory-access semantics to identify reusable 
%fusion regions, maps them to parameterized tiled templates for GEMM, convolution, and 
%common fused epilogues, and selects high-performance kernel configurations with a 
%lightweight trait-driven analytical model. 
This design decouples shape-invariant 
fusion decisions from shape-dependent tuning choices, allowing fused kernels to adapt
efficiently to varying sequence lengths, batch sizes, and hardware characteristics. 
Experiments on A100 and H100 GPUs across operator-level and end-to-end Transformer/CNN 
workloads show that MAGSAT consistently outperforms the state-of-the-art baseline.
